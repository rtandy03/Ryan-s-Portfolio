Automated and Underprepared

We have all heard the rumors about how Artificial Intelligence is "making us all dumber", but how true really is that statement? Using datasets collected by Kaggle and Stanford, consisting of data tracking AI adoption rates as well as state average test scores from 2018 to 2024, this report shows the relationship between increased AI adoption and learning.

While correlation does not imply causation, some patters emerge that suggest a strong negative relationship between the two variables; meaning the higher AI adoption rate, the lower the average test scores.
